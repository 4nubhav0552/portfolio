## 🌟 Executive Overview & Design System

This portfolio and executive resume feature a handcrafted **Real Paper & Sketchbook Edition** — pairing authentic physical paper textures (`#FDFBF7`), organic multi-layer drop shadows, warm fiber grain, and natural handwritten typography (`Caveat` & `Patrick Hand`) with an **interactive animated pencil cursor** and fading graphite sketch trails.

### 🎨 Key Design Decisions
1. **Real Paper Texture & Physical Cards:**
   - **Texture & Lighting:** Cards have embedded SVG paper fiber noise, subtle warm borders (`#E5DFD5`), and multi-stage physical drop shadows with warm inner edge ambient lighting.
   - **Tactile Micro-Angles:** Cards subtly angle (`rotate(-0.35deg)` / `rotate(0.35deg)`) on hover as if physical sheets of paper are being lifted from a strategist's desk.
   - **Sticky Note Badges:** Badges are designed like cheerful sticky tape tabs (`#FEF9C3`) with natural rotation and handwriting.
2. **Handwritten Typography:**
   - Headings and titles styled with **`Caveat`** (weights 600-700) for natural, fluid handwritten character.
   - Paragraphs and card details formatted in **`Patrick Hand`** for high legibility while retaining authentic handwritten charm.
3. **Animated Pencil Cursor & Sketch Trail:**
   - **Custom Pencil Cursor:** Custom SVG pencil with pink eraser, metal ferrule, yellow hex wood, and a dark graphite lead tip.
   - **Live Graphite Sketch Trail:** Moving the mouse renders a natural graphite line on the canvas that gently fades out.
   - **Tactile Tap & Lead Dust:** Clicking taps the pencil on the paper and puffs tiny graphite dust particles!
4. **Professional Portrait Framing:**
   - The headshot is displayed in crisp paper-framed borders across the navigation bar, hero cockpit, and executive profile sections.
5. **Interactive Growth & ROAS Simulator:**
   - An interactive pencil-styled growth calculator allowing hiring managers and founders to project Clicks, Conversions, CPA, Revenue, and ROAS.
6. **Strategic Campaign Blueprints (Playbooks):**
   - Interactive notebook tabs for Google Search SEM, YouTube Video Action Funnels, and Automated Reporting Pipelines.
7. **Print-to-PDF Resume Feature:**
   - Standalone executive resume page ([`resume.html`](resume.html)) with 1-click A4 print formatting.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Double-click
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Python)
```bash
python -m http.server 3000
# Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser
```

### Option 3: Node / NPX
```bash
npx serve .
```
